References back to ToC

The NORMA ontology is informed by the following standards, vocabularies, and background references.

  • Leyva-Sánchez, S. et al. (2026). NORMA-O: The NORMA Ontology for Legal Norm Annotations (Version 1.0) [Ontology]. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19765302.
  • European Legislation Identifier (ELI) ontology. Publications Office of the European Union. Available at: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli-register/about.html
  • Lebo, T., Sahoo, S., and McGuinness, D. PROV-O: The PROV Ontology. W3C Recommendation. Available at: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/
  • Miles, A., and Bechhofer, S., eds. SKOS Simple Knowledge Organization System Reference. W3C Recommendation. Available at: https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/
  • Hitzler, P., Krötzsch, M., Parsia, B., Patel-Schneider, P. F., and Rudolph, S., eds. OWL 2 Web Ontology Language Primer (Second Edition). W3C Recommendation. Available at: https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-primer/
  • Noy, N., and Rector, A. Defining N-ary Relations on the Semantic Web. W3C Working Group Note. Available at: https://www.w3.org/TR/swbp-n-aryRelations/
  • Brickley, D., and Miller, L. FOAF Vocabulary Specification. Available at: http://xmlns.com/foaf/spec/

These references are especially relevant for understanding the ontology's provenance model, its treatment of legal resources, its use of SKOS-based controlled vocabularies, and its adoption of the n-ary relation pattern for branch-sensitive conditional activation.
