Abstract

NORMA is an OWL ontology for representing legal norm annotations as structured, reusable knowledge graph resources. It covers the core elements of annotated regulatory knowledge: the distinction between regulative norms and constitutive rules; the legal agents, actions, objects, conditions, and sources involved; conditional activation of normative content through a reified trigger-event pattern; and provenance and lifecycle metadata for traceability and accountability. Controlled vocabularies are provided for binding force, compliance criticality, review status, extraction method, and lifecycle state.

The ontology aligns with established standards: PROV-O for provenance, ELI for legal resource modelling, FOAF for agent metadata, and SKOS for concept schemes. It is intended for use in annotation pipelines built on the NORMA toolkit, and supports Linked Data publication, SHACL validation, SPARQL querying, and compliance-oriented reasoning applications.
