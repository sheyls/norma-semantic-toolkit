Abstract

This ontology is the ABox (assertional component) of the EU AI Act knowledge graph, produced by the NORMA semantic toolkit from structured BPMN-based norm annotations. It instantiates the NORMA ontology (norma-o) with the concrete legal individuals extracted from the EU AI Act, covering legal agents, legal actions, legal objects, legal sources, normative content items, and the trigger–event structures that govern their conditional applicability. The result is a machine-readable, SPARQL-queryable representation of EU AI Act obligations, prohibitions, and permissions, ready for integration with compliance tooling, knowledge graph publishing, and automated reasoning pipelines.
