EU AI Act ABox: Description back to ToC

This ontology is the assertional component (ABox) of the EU AI Act knowledge graph. It is automatically generated by the NORMA pipeline from the BPMN-based annotation diagrams maintained in the regulations/eu-ai-act/bpmn/ directory and populated using the NORMA ontology (https://w3id.org/def/norma-o) as its TBox.

Content

The ABox represents EU AI Act normative content as a set of populated NORMA individuals:

  • Legal agents — roles identified in the regulation, such as AI providers, deployers, importers, and notified bodies;
  • Legal actions — the acts required, permitted, or prohibited under the regulation;
  • Legal objects — the systems, data, and artefacts that are the subject of regulation, including AI systems, general-purpose AI models, and high-risk AI systems;
  • Legal sources — references to the specific articles and recitals of the EU AI Act from which each annotation was derived;
  • Normative content items — instantiated obligations, prohibitions, and permissions, each linked to its legal context, deontic classification, binding force, and compliance criticality;
  • Trigger–event structures — reified n-ary relationships connecting condition outcomes to the normative content items they activate, enabling conditional applicability to be queried and reasoned over explicitly.

Generation and provenance

All individuals in this ontology were produced by the NORMA semantic pipeline (norma build) and carry full PROV-O provenance metadata. Each annotation records its extraction method, review status, and source traceability back to the originating BPMN diagram and EU AI Act article. The ABox imports the NORMA ontology and is intended to be loaded together with it for validation, querying, and downstream reasoning.
