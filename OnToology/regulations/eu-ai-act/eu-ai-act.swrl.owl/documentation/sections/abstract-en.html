Abstract

This ontology contains the SWRL rule layer for the EU AI Act knowledge graph. It extends the EU AI Act ABox with sixteen executable SWRL rules that encode the conditional logic of the regulation: given a set of boolean properties describing an AI system's characteristics, each rule fires the norma:activatesNorm property to connect the relevant trigger–event to the applicable normative content item. The rules operationalise compliance decision paths for obligations and prohibitions covering high-risk AI systems, general-purpose AI models, biometric identification, real-time surveillance, cybersecurity exemptions, and criminal offence prediction, enabling automated reasoning engines to determine which EU AI Act norms apply to a specific system configuration.
